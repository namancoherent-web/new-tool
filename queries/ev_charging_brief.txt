Identify and provide a validated list of at least 150 independent, relevant, non-overlapping companies operating in the Global Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure Market.

Market scope: companies that manufacture, install, own/operate, or provide software/network services for EV charging stations and charging infrastructure (AC and DC fast chargers), for public, workplace, fleet, and home charging use cases.

Player Selection Criteria
Include only companies directly involved in:
- Manufacturing EV charging hardware (chargers, connectors, charging cabinets)
- Owning/operating public or fleet charging networks (charge point operators)
- Providing charging management software/network platforms
- Providing charging-as-a-service or turnkey charging infrastructure solutions

Strictly Exclude. Do NOT include:
- Electric vehicle manufacturers themselves (OEMs) unless they also independently manufacture or operate charging infrastructure as a distinct business
- Battery cell/pack manufacturers
- General electrical component distributors with no specific EV charging product
- Utility companies unless they operate a distinct EV charging brand/subsidiary
- Companies that only make traditional (non-EV) electrical equipment
- Real estate companies that merely host chargers without owning/operating the charging business

Independence & Non-Overlap Rules
- Do not list the same company more than once
- Do not list a subsidiary separately from its parent if they represent the same charging business
- Do not list a brand separately from the company that owns it
- Validate that each player has a specific, verifiable connection to EV charging infrastructure
