Identify a list of at least 100 independent Indian companies manufacturing and selling Ayurvedic and herbal personal care / wellness products (skincare, haircare, herbal supplements, and traditional wellness formulations) in India.

Include companies that:
- Manufacture and sell their own branded Ayurvedic/herbal skincare, haircare, or wellness products
- Operate primarily or significantly within India
- Have an established retail or D2C presence (not just a single local shop with no brand)

Exclude:
- Pure raw-herb/ingredient suppliers with no finished consumer product
- International (non-Indian) companies, even if they sell Ayurvedic-labeled products
- Generic pharmaceutical companies with no Ayurvedic/herbal product line
- E-commerce marketplaces or retailers that only distribute other brands' products
- Companies with no verifiable Ayurvedic/herbal product portfolio

Do not list the same company twice under different spellings. Do not list a brand separately from its parent company.
