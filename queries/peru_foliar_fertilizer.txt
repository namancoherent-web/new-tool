Identify 150-200 independent, relevant, non-overlapping companies operating in the Peru Foliar Fertilizer Market.

Scope: foliar fertilizers applied directly to plant foliage in Peru, including conventional, chelated, bio-based, organic, macro-, secondary- and micronutrient foliar fertilizer products used across agricultural crops, horticulture, turf and ornamentals. This covers nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, calcium, magnesium, sulfur, zinc, boron, iron, manganese and copper foliar products, in liquid, powder or water-soluble solid form, across cereals, fruits and vegetables, oilseeds and pulses, plantation crops, and turf and ornamentals.

INCLUDE only companies directly manufacturing, producing, formulating, supplying or commercially selling foliar fertilizers or foliar-applied nutrient products relevant to Peru. Each company must have a specific, verifiable connection to foliar fertilizer products, not merely general fertilizer or agricultural inputs. Prioritise manufacturers of macro-, micro- and secondary-nutrient foliar fertilizers, producers of chelated foliar fertilizers, manufacturers of bio-based and organic foliar fertilizers, specialty fertilizer companies with dedicated foliar nutrition portfolios, agricultural input companies with specific foliar fertilizer products, and international fertilizer companies with established commercial operations or supply in Peru.

EXCLUDE: soil-only fertilizer companies with no foliar portfolio; generic fertilizer manufacturers without specific foliar products; seed companies; crop protection and pesticide companies without foliar products; biostimulant companies without a clear foliar fertilizer product; agricultural machinery, equipment and irrigation companies; raw-material suppliers without finished foliar products; packaging and logistics companies; retailers and marketplaces; distributors/dealers that only resell another company's products with no proprietary or manufacturing activity; farms that only use foliar fertilizers; research institutions, associations, consultants and market research firms; companies with no verifiable foliar fertilizer product.

PERU RELEVANCE: the company must have demonstrable manufacturing, sales, distribution, commercial or established supply presence in Peru for foliar fertilizer products. Companies headquartered outside Peru qualify if their foliar fertilizer products are commercially supplied or sold in Peru. Do not include a company solely because it operates globally in fertilizers.

INDEPENDENCE: list each company once only. Do not list a parent company and subsidiary separately when they are the same foliar fertilizer business, do not list a brand separately from its owner, do not list multiple brands owned by the same company as separate players, do not list duplicate companies under different names or regional entities, and do not list distributors merely because they distribute another company's products. If a company operates across multiple fertilizer categories, list it only once.
