Player Selection Criteria
Include only companies that are directly involved in manufacturing, producing, or owning brands of food thin wafers / wafer biscuits.

Prioritize:
- Companies manufacturing thin wafer biscuits
- Companies producing cream-filled wafer biscuits
- Companies producing chocolate-coated wafer biscuits
- Companies producing plain or specialty wafer biscuits
- Companies with branded wafer products
- Companies with private-label / contract manufacturing capabilities specifically for wafer products
- Companies with established domestic or international sales of food wafers

Strictly Exclude. Do NOT include:
- Raw-material suppliers
- Flour, cocoa, sugar, cream, chocolate or ingredient suppliers
- Wafer-making machinery/equipment manufacturers
- Packaging companies
- Logistics providers
- Distributors without their own wafer manufacturing
- Retailers and supermarkets
- E-commerce marketplaces
- Foodservice distributors without manufacturing
- Companies that only sell wafer-making equipment
- Companies manufacturing semiconductor/electronic wafers
- Silicon wafer manufacturers
- Glass/ceramic wafer manufacturers
- Pharmaceutical wafer manufacturers
- Ice-cream cone manufacturers unless they also manufacture food thin wafer biscuits
- Companies whose wafer products are only incidental and not commercially relevant
- Companies with no verifiable wafer product portfolio

Independence & Non-Overlap Rules. Each company must be an independent player/entity.
- Do not list the same company more than once.
- Do not list a parent company and its wholly owned subsidiary separately when they represent the same wafer business.
- Do not list a brand separately from the company that owns/manufactures it.
- Do not list multiple subsidiaries of the same corporate group unless they operate as genuinely independent wafer manufacturers in different markets.
- Do not list distributors that merely distribute another company's wafer brands.
- Do not list duplicate companies under different spellings or regional names.
- Do not include companies simply because they appear in a generic biscuit/confectionery database.
- Validate that each player has a specific and verifiable connection to food thin wafers / wafer biscuits.
